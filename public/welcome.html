When several sources tell the story

Dated snapshot · Friday 28 August 2026

Caribbean Grand Market at CARIFESTA House

The question “I'm looking for something cultural to do. What's going on this week?”

Ten days of regional makers from Grenada, Jamaica, Guyana, Suriname, Trinidad & Tobago and Barbados, with fashion, art, craft, wellness products and a food court. Free admission. Open 10 am–10 pm daily.

  • 98.1 The One (radio)
  • VOB 92.9 (radio)
  • The Beat 104.1 (radio)
  • National Cultural Foundation Instagram (organiser)
  • Two visitor TikToks
  • Attendee Instagram photos

What this shows: six sources mostly agreed on venue, dates and hours. One visitor TikTok disagreed on the closing date (31 vs 30 August), so Pulse reported both windows and flagged which was the safer recommendation.

When the evidence is on the radio

Recorded example · 6 September 2026

Caribbean Premier League at Kensington Oval

The question “What's happening with cricket in Barbados?”

The CPL is live at Kensington Oval. The Beat 104.1 FM is the official radio rights holder for the tournament, and Q 100.7 FM is carrying live ball-by-ball commentary alongside CBC TV8. LIFE 97.5 confirms the bigger picture: T20 cricket runs through August and September, with the playoffs and the final at Kensington Oval.

  • The Beat 104.1 FM (rights holder, live commentary)
  • Q 100.7 FM (CBC TV8 broadcast promo)
  • LIFE 97.5 FM (schedule read)
  • VOB 92.9 (cricket discussion)

What this shows: for live events, every source is the radio broadcast itself. There is no article to link to, the audio IS the evidence. Play any radio card in the app to hear the original broadcast.
