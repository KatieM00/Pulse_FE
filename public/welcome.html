01 / When a listing is out of date

Suarez Brothers Circus at Garrison Savannah

Historical example · 28 August 2026

“I'm looking for something different to do with my family tonight. What's going on?”

The circus was running ten days of family shows, but an older listing pointed to Belle Junction. Pulse found Garrison Savannah, 7 pm in newer radio broadcasts, with visitor TikToks supporting the change of venue.

  • Q 100.7 FM and The Beat 104.1 FM broadcasts
  • Recent visitor TikToks
  • An older, superseded event listing

02 / When several sources tell the story

Caribbean Grand Market at CARIFESTA House

Historical example · 28 August 2026

“I'm looking for something cultural to do. What's going on this week?”

Regional makers, food, fashion and craft came together at CARIFESTA House. Radio coverage and the organiser's Instagram pointed to free admission and daily opening from 10 am to 10 pm.

A visitor TikTok gave a different closing date. Rather than hide that disagreement, Pulse reported both dates and recommended the safer 29–30 August weekend window.

  • 98.1 The One and other local radio coverage
  • National Cultural Foundation Instagram
  • Visitor TikToks and attendee Instagram photos

What this showsSeveral sources can build a fuller picture without pretending every detail agrees.

03 / When the evidence is on the radio

Caribbean Premier League at Kensington Oval

Historical example · 6 September 2026

“What's happening with cricket in Barbados?”

The Beat 104.1 FM's tournament coverage and commentary placed the CPL at Kensington Oval. Q 100.7 promoted the CBC broadcast, while LIFE 97.5's schedule read confirmed the wider August–September programme, including the playoffs and final.

There wasn't a verified visitor TikTok or Instagram post for this fixture in the answer. Pulse made that gap explicit: the strongest evidence was the broadcast itself.

  • The Beat 104.1 FM: rights-holder announcement and commentary
  • LIFE 97.5: tournament schedule
  • Q 100.7 FM: CBC Sports broadcast promo

What this showsSometimes there is no article to link to. The audio is the evidence, and you can play the original clips in the app.
