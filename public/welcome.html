When a listing is out of date

Dated snapshot · Friday 28 August 2026

🎪 Suarez Brothers Circus · Garrison Savannah

The question: “I'm looking for something different to do with my family tonight. What's going on?”

The biggest circus from Mexico is running ten days of family shows. Pulse answers Garrison Savannah, 7 pm — not Belle Junction — because Q 100.7 FM and The Beat 104.1 FM both confirmed the move mid-run, and recent visitor TikToks agree.

  • Q 100.7 FM broadcast
  • The Beat 104.1 FM broadcast
  • Two TikTok visitor posts
  • 1 superseded event listing (Belle Junction)

What this shows: when an older listing and newer evidence disagree, Pulse explains the change rather than silently picking one source.

When several sources tell the story

Dated snapshot · Friday 28 August 2026

🎉 Caribbean Grand Market · CARIFESTA House, Waterford

The question: “I'm looking for something cultural to do. What's going on this week?”

Ten days of regional makers (Grenada, Jamaica, Guyana, Suriname, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados), fashion, art and craft, wellness products, food court. Free admission. Open 10 am–10 pm daily.

  • 98.1 The One (radio)
  • VOB 92.9 (radio)
  • The Beat 104.1 (radio)
  • National Cultural Foundation Instagram (organiser)
  • Two visitor TikToks
  • Attendee Instagram photos

What this shows: six sources mostly agreed on venue, dates and hours — one visitor TikTok disagreed on the closing date (31 vs 30 August). Pulse reported both windows and flagged which was the safer recommendation.

When the evidence is on the radio

Recorded example · 6 September 2026

🏏 Caribbean Premier League at Kensington Oval

The question: “What's happening with cricket in Barbados?”

The CPL is live at Kensington Oval. The Beat 104.1 FM is the official radio rights holder for the tournament, and Q 100.7 FM is carrying live ball-by-ball commentary alongside CBC TV8. LIFE 97.5 confirms the bigger picture: T20 cricket runs through August and September with the playoffs and the final at Kensington Oval.

  • The Beat 104.1 FM (rights holder, live commentary)
  • Q 100.7 FM (CBC TV8 broadcast promo)
  • LIFE 97.5 FM (schedule read)
  • VOB 92.9 (cricket discussion)

What this shows: for live events, every source IS the radio broadcast. There's no article to link to — the audio is the evidence. Play any radio card in the app to hear the original broadcast.
